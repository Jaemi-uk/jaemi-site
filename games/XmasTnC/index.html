Christmas Giveaway – Official Rules

At a glance

Comment on our Christmas film for a chance to win a mystery Christmas gift. It is free to enter and open worldwide to people aged 18 or over, except in Italy, Brazil and the Canadian province of Québec (because of local giveaway laws) and anywhere else it is prohibited by law.

The full rules below apply. By entering, you agree to them.

1. Promoter

The promoter is Jaemi.uk, which runs Jamie's Jazz Rooms, the YouTube channel at youtube.com/@jamiesjazzrooms ("we", "us").

  • Promoter: Jaemi.uk. Our postal address is available on request by email.
  • Contact email for this giveaway: admin@jaemi.uk

The contact email above is the only address we use for this giveaway.

2. Eligibility

  1. You must be aged 18 or over when you enter. We may ask for proof of age before giving the prize.
  2. The giveaway is open worldwide, except where it is prohibited or restricted by law. Because local laws require prize promotions there to be registered or run locally, it is not open to residents of Italy, Brazil or the Canadian province of Québec. Entries from these places will not be counted. It is also not open to residents of any country or region subject to UK, EU, UN or US sanctions that would stop us sending the prize. If the winner lives in Canada, they must correctly answer a short maths skill-testing question by email before receiving the prize.
  3. Our team, the people who help run the channel, and their immediate families cannot enter.
  4. You need a YouTube account to post a comment. You must follow YouTube's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  5. You do not need to subscribe, like, share, or buy anything to enter or to win.

3. Promotion period

The giveaway opens at 21:00 UK time (GMT) on 17 November 2026 and closes at 23:59 UK time (GMT) on 10 December 2026. Comments posted outside these times do not count. The time shown by YouTube on the comment decides whether it arrived in time.

4. How to enter

  1. Watch the full film Christmas Jazz Around the World on the Jamie's Jazz Rooms channel.
  2. Post a comment on that film telling us your favourite song in the film and who you would play it for.
  3. Only comments on that full film count. Comments on our Shorts, other videos or social media do not count.

Rules for entries:

  • One entry per person. If you post more than one comment, only your first comment counts. Replies to other comments do not count.
  • Your comment must be your own original words, in any language.
  • Entries that are offensive, hateful, misleading, spam, copied from someone else, or written with automated tools are disqualified.
  • If you delete your comment, or YouTube removes it, your entry is withdrawn.
  • We are not responsible for comments that do not appear because of YouTube's spam filters or technical problems.

5. How the winner is chosen

This is a free prize draw. After the closing date, we collect every valid entry and pick one winner at random with an online random comment picker that gives verifiably random results. A person who is not connected to the channel watches the draw, and we record the screen, so the result can be checked.

We also draw two reserve entries, in order, in case the winner cannot be contacted or is not eligible. Every valid entry has the same chance of winning, whatever it says.

6. Prize

There is one prize: a mystery Christmas gift worth £25 (GBP), chosen by us, plus free tracked shipping.

  • We aim to send the prize by 18 December 2026. Delivery times depend on the winner's country, and we cannot promise delivery before Christmas.
  • If we cannot ship a physical gift to the winner's country, we may give a digital gift card of equal value instead.
  • The prize cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to someone else.
  • The winner is responsible for any import duties, customs fees or taxes in their country.
  • If the prize becomes unavailable, we may replace it with a prize of equal or greater value.

7. Winner announcement and claiming the prize

  1. On 11 December 2026 we announce the winner by replying to the winning comment and in a pinned comment on the film. We publish the winner's YouTube name and country. If you win and would rather we show only your YouTube name, tell us when you email us. We also reply to the winning comment again 2 days later, as a reminder.
  2. To claim the prize, the winner must email our contact email (section 1) within 5 days of the announcement, from 11 December to 16 December 2026, with:
    • their full name
    • their full delivery address
    • a contact phone number (needed for the courier)
    • their YouTube channel name, so we can confirm the entry is theirs
  3. Never post your name, address or phone number in a public comment.
  4. If the winner does not reply by 16 December 2026, cannot prove they posted the winning comment, or is not eligible, they lose the prize. We then offer it to the first reserve entry, then the second, each with 2 days to reply.
  5. If no one claims the prize, we may keep it.

8. Privacy

We only collect personal details from the winner (and reserves, if needed): their name, address, phone number and email.

  • We use these details only to confirm the winner and deliver the prize.
  • We share them only with the delivery company or gift-card provider, for delivery.
  • We delete them within 60 days of delivery, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • We do not sell them, and we do not use them for marketing.

We process these details under UK data protection law (UK GDPR). You can ask us to see or delete your details at any time by writing to our contact email.

9. Scam warning

Scammers often pretend to be channels running giveaways. Remember:

  • We announce the winner only on our own channel, by replying from the official Jamie's Jazz Rooms account.
  • We never ask for money, fees, card or bank details, passwords or verification codes.
  • We never ask you to message us on WhatsApp, Telegram or any other app.
  • We only contact the winner through our official email (section 1), after the winner has written to us first.

If anyone contacts you about this giveaway in another way, it is a scam. Do not reply, and report it to YouTube.
